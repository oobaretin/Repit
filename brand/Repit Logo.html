Design rationale

01
The gap. An open ring, not a closed one. Represents the pause between repetitions — the space where mindfulness lives.
02
The cap. The luminous dot at the ring's start is the "seed" of the next rep. It gives the mark a subtle sense of motion without animating.
03
Gradient discipline. Gradient lives only on the mark, angled 135°. The base stays a flat, cool slate so the icon feels grounded — not decorative.
04
Small-size legibility. Stroke weight is 11% of the icon width — heavy enough to survive at 40pt notification size without becoming clunky at 1024.
